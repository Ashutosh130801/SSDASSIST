# Field Officer — turn on always‑on location (2 minutes, once)

Do this **once** after installing the app so your live location keeps sharing even when the
phone is **locked, in your pocket, or while you're using another app** (WhatsApp, Maps, calls).
If you skip these, the phone will stop tracking when the screen turns off or you switch apps.

## Step 1 — Log in
Open the app and log in. When it asks:
- **Location** → tap **Allow**
- **Notifications** → tap **Allow**

You'll see an **"RecoverIQ — on duty"** notification. That notification means tracking is ON.
Do **not** swipe it away.

## Step 2 — Set location to "Allow all the time" (most important)
Settings → **Apps → RecoverIQ → Permissions → Location** →
- Choose **"Allow all the time"**
- Turn **"Use precise location"** ON

(The app will usually open this screen for you — just pick "Allow all the time".)

## Step 3 — Stop the phone from killing it
Settings → **Apps → RecoverIQ → Battery** → set to **Unrestricted** / **"Don't optimize"**.

Then your phone brand's extra step:

| Phone brand | Do this |
|-------------|---------|
| **Xiaomi / Redmi / POCO** | Apps → RecoverIQ → **Autostart: ON**; Battery saver → **No restrictions**. In Recent apps, **lock** the RecoverIQ card. |
| **Realme / Oppo / OnePlus** | Apps → RecoverIQ → **Auto‑launch / Allow auto start: ON**; **Allow background activity**. |
| **Vivo / iQOO** | Battery → **High background power** → allow RecoverIQ; **Auto‑start: ON**. |
| **Samsung** | Battery → **Background usage limits** → add RecoverIQ to **"Never sleeping apps"**. |
| **Motorola / Nokia / Pixel (stock Android)** | Just Step 3 (Unrestricted) is enough. |

## Step 4 — Check it works
Lock the phone, walk ~50 metres, then your manager opens **Field Tracking** — your green **● Live**
dot should move within ~15 seconds.

---

**Two rules to remember**
1. Keep the **"on duty" notification** — don't clear it.
2. If your manager says your location stopped, it's almost always **Battery/Autostart** (Step 3)
   — re‑check those.
