# RecoverIQ — Training Video Script & Storyboard

A record-ready script for a staff training video covering the **web app** and the **Android app**.
Each scene lists what to show on screen, the action to perform, and the exact narration to read.

**How to use this**
- Screen-record the web on a computer (OBS Studio, free) and the app on a phone (built-in screen
  recorder, or scrcpy to mirror the phone to the PC).
- Read the **Narration** lines aloud (or use a voiceover). Keep a calm, friendly pace.
- Record with a **demo/test account per role** so nothing sensitive is shown.
- Target length: **~10 minutes** total. You can also export just one role's section as a short clip.
- Tip: pair this with `RecoverIQ_Staff_Training.pptx` — you can screen-record the slides for the
  intro/outro and switch to the live app for the "show me" parts.

Legend — **[SCREEN]** what's visible · **[DO]** the action to perform · **[SAY]** narration.

## MODULE 0 — Intro  (~40 sec)

**Scene 0.1**
- [SCREEN] Title slide of the presentation (RecoverIQ — Staff Training).
- [SAY] "Welcome to RecoverIQ — the SSD Enterprises collections platform. In the next few
  minutes you'll learn how to log in, set up your profile, and use the screens for your role.
  RecoverIQ works two ways: on the web in any browser, and as a mobile app. Both share the same
  live data — so a payment logged on the phone shows up instantly on the web."

**Scene 0.2**
- [SCREEN] Slide showing the six roles.
- [SAY] "The screens you see depend on your role — Admin and Head Office, Manager, Team Lead,
  Tele-caller, Field Officer, and HR. Skip ahead to your role if you like."

---

## MODULE 1 — First login & your E-ID  (everyone, ~1.5 min)

**Scene 1.1 — Signing in (web)**
- [SCREEN] The web login page.
- [DO] Type the email and the starter password `Ssd@2026`; click Sign in.
- [SAY] "Open the site in your browser and sign in with the email your admin gave you and the
  starter password Ssd@2026."

**Scene 1.2 — Set your own password**
- [SCREEN] The "Set a new password" screen that appears on first login.
- [DO] Enter a new password twice; continue.
- [SAY] "The very first time, you must create your own private password. Choose something only
  you know — never share it."

**Scene 1.3 — Your E-ID**
- [SCREEN] The My E-ID screen (ID card with photo, code, role, branch).
- [DO] Click "Change photo" and pick an image; then open the one-time details form and fill a
  couple of fields; Save.
- [SAY] "This is your E-ID — your digital ID card. Add your photo any time, and fill in your
  personal details once. After you save, the details lock; if something changes later, HR can
  update it for you."

**Scene 1.4 — Same on the app**
- [SCREEN] The app login → forced password change → My E-ID.
- [SAY] "It's exactly the same in the mobile app: sign in, set your password, and your E-ID is
  right there in the menu."

## MODULE 2 — Your role

> Record only the sections relevant to your audience, or all of them for a full library.

### 2A — Admin & Head Office  (~1 min)
- [SCREEN] Web, logged in as admin. Show the left menu.
- [SAY] "Admin and Head Office see everything across all branches."
- [DO] Click **Accounts / Upload** → open the upload form → pick bank, product, month → choose a
  file with CALLER ID, FOS ID, TEAM LEAD ID columns → Import.
- [SAY] "Your main job is uploading case files. Choose the bank, product and month, pick the
  Excel, and upload. Because the sheet has the employee-ID columns, every case is automatically
  assigned to the right caller, field officer and team lead. After it finishes, you get a report
  showing what imported and flags any IDs that didn't match."
- [DO] Open **MIS**.
- [SAY] "MIS shows live performance — recovery, targets versus achieved, leaderboards — and you
  can download any of it to Excel."
- [DO] Open **Field Tracking** → click **FOS roster**.
- [SAY] "Field Tracking is the live map. The FOS roster button shows who's active or inactive
  today, or on any past date you pick."
- [DO] Open **Manpower**.
- [SAY] "And Manpower is your staff directory — search anyone, add or edit staff."

### 2B — Manager  (~45 sec)
- [SCREEN] Web, manager account.
- [SAY] "A Manager runs one branch. Everything you see is scoped to your branch."
- [DO] Show **Dashboard** and **MIS**.
- [SAY] "Your dashboard and MIS show your branch's recovery, pending and targets."
- [DO] Open an account from **Accounts** → show Call, WhatsApp, Record payment, Log call.
- [SAY] "Open any account to call the customer, send a WhatsApp, record a payment, or log a
  follow-up."
- [DO] Open **Field Tracking** and **Team**.
- [SAY] "Track your field team on the map, and manage your staff and approve leave under Team."

### 2C — Team Lead  (~40 sec)
- [SCREEN] Web or app, team lead account.
- [DO] Open **My Team**.
- [SAY] "As a Team Lead you see each of your members — their activity and collections today."
- [DO] Open **Team Accounts** → show call buttons (customer / caller / FOS).
- [SAY] "Team Accounts are all the cases assigned to your team. You can call the customer, or
  call your caller or field officer directly."
- [DO] Open **PTP Tracker** and show **Escalate**.
- [SAY] "Chase promise-to-pay dates in the PTP tracker, and escalate a stuck case to yourself
  without hurting your member's numbers."

### 2D — Tele-caller  (~1 min)
- [SCREEN] Web, tele-caller account.
- [DO] Open **Calling** → tap a customer to call → log a disposition (PTP with amount + date).
- [SAY] "Tele-callers work the Calling queue. Tap a customer to call, then log what happened —
  a promise to pay, paid, or a follow-up. Always log it: that's your performance record."
- [DO] Open **Live Sheet** → edit a cell (amount/remarks).
- [SAY] "The Live Sheet works like Excel — edit amounts, status and remarks inline, saved live."
- [DO] Open a case → enter a **New phone / New address** → Save.
- [SAY] "If you find a new phone number or address for a customer, add it here. The assigned
  field officer is notified instantly."

### 2E — Field Officer (FOS)  (~1.5 min, mostly the app)
- [SCREEN] The app home screen.
- [DO] Flip the **On Duty** switch on.
- [SAY] "Field Officers mostly use the app. When you start your day, turn on the On-Duty switch —
  this shares your live location with your branch, even in the background."
- [DO] Open **My Accounts** → show a case → tap **Navigate**, then **Call**, then **WhatsApp**.
- [SAY] "Open My Accounts to see your customers. One tap gives you directions, a call, or a
  WhatsApp message."
- [DO] Open a case → **Log visit** → attach a photo, confirm location, enter amount → Save.
- [SAY] "After a visit, log it: add a photo, your GPS location is captured automatically, and
  enter any amount collected."
- [DO] Show a **notification** for an updated address; tap it → the highlighted contact card →
  Navigate.
- [SAY] "If a caller finds a new address or phone, you get a notification and a highlighted card —
  tap Navigate to go straight there."
- [DO] Show the **Update available** prompt (if visible).
- [SAY] "When a new app version is ready, you'll see an Update prompt — tap Update to stay current."

### 2F — HR  (~45 sec)
- [SCREEN] Web, HR account → **Manpower**.
- [DO] Type in the search box (name / code / phone).
- [SAY] "HR manages the people directory. Search anyone by name, code, phone, designation or
  branch."
- [DO] Click **Add staff** → fill the form → Save.
- [SAY] "Add a new employee with their full details — a role-wise employee code and login are
  created automatically."
- [DO] Open an employee card → **Edit** → change a field / reset password → Save.
- [SAY] "Open anyone's card and click Edit to update their details, reset a password, or change
  their role. You can also download the whole directory to Excel."

## MODULE 3 — Key workflows everyone should know  (~1.5 min)

**Scene 3.1 — Upload → auto-allocation**
- [SCREEN] The upload report after a commit.
- [SAY] "Remember: uploaded cases allocate themselves using the ID columns. The report after
  each upload tells you exactly which rows couldn't be matched, so bad names or typos get caught."

**Scene 3.2 — New address/phone alert (the full loop)**
- [SCREEN] Split idea: caller enters new phone on web → FOS phone shows a notification.
- [SAY] "When a caller records a new phone or address, the field officer is alerted immediately —
  keeping everyone working from the latest information."

**Scene 3.3 — Live tracking & roster**
- [SCREEN] Field Tracking live map + FOS roster popup with a date picker.
- [SAY] "Managers and admins can watch the team live, replay any officer's route for a past day,
  and check the active-versus-inactive roster for any date."

**Scene 3.4 — Leave & Security**
- [SCREEN] Leave screen, then Security screen.
- [SAY] "Everyone can apply for leave and see their balance. Under Security, change your password,
  turn on two-step verification, or add a fingerprint or Face ID login. New devices need admin
  approval — that keeps your account safe."

**Scene 3.5 — Getting the app & staying updated**
- [SCREEN] The login page "Download Android app" button.
- [SAY] "To get the mobile app, tap Download Android app on the login page and install it. On an
  iPhone, open the site in Safari and choose Add to Home Screen."

---

## MODULE 4 — Closing  (~20 sec)

**Scene 4.1**
- [SCREEN] Closing slide (web address + app download).
- [SAY] "That's it. Log in, complete your E-ID, and explore the menu for your role. If you ever
  get stuck — a forgotten password or a new device — your team lead or admin can help. Welcome
  to RecoverIQ."
